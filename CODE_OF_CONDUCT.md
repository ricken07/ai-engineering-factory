# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive community. Participants should:

- be considerate and professional;
- welcome different viewpoints and experience levels;
- focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and unconstructive disruption.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers through the project's private GitHub reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
